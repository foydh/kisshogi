# Assets and provenance

## Original Kisshogi assets

The following are original works created for Kisshogi and are dedicated to the
public domain under **CC0 1.0**:

- The CSS pentagon piece design and wood/grid board design.
- `src/kisshogi/web/assets/logo.svg`.
- Inline interface icons and the application screenshots in `docs/`.
- Any other CSS artwork in `src/kisshogi/web/css/`.

They contain no third-party game artwork.

## Fonts

The app bundles small subsets of Noto CJK fonts:

- `pieces.woff2` — Noto Serif CJK JP.
- `ui-ja.woff2` — Noto Sans CJK JP.
- `ui-zh-CN.woff2` — Noto Sans CJK SC.

Noto CJK is Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/) and Google Inc.,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full license text is in
`src/kisshogi/web/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. The subsets are generated by
`tools/build_fonts.py` from locally installed Noto CJK fonts; the font files
are embedded in the application and are not otherwise modified.

`src/kisshogi/web/vendor/tsshogi/LICENSE` and

No ShogiHome code, piece images, board textures, icons, or screenshots are used
